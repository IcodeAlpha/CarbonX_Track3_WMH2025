## Problem Statement

### No Proof of Real Action

- People plant trees, install solar panels, use clean cookstoves **but there's no way to prove these actions happened**
- Claims of environmental work cannot be verified and **impact remains invisible**

### Fragmented Data & Reporting

- NGOs, counties, and governments **lack real-time ground data** and there is **no centralized system** to measure national climate impact
- **Impossible to track progress** toward SDGs and climate goals

### Kenya's Climate Commitments Require Accurate Tracking:

- **NDC targets:** 32% emissions reduction by 2030
- **SDG 13:** Climate action with measurable indicators

### The Gap

**Between what people do and what can be proven, measured, and rewarded.**

## Our Solution

# CarbonX: AI-Verified Climate Community Platform

### What We Built:

A digital platform that transforms individual climate actions into verifiable, measurable impact through:

### Core Innovation: Triple Verification

1. **AI Analysis** - Image recognition validates actions
2. **Geolocation** - GPS coordinates prove location authenticity
3. **Community Engagement** - Social verification and tracking

### User Journey:

```
Select Action Type → Pin GPS Location → Add Details & Photos → 
AI Verification → Earn Climate Tokens
```

### Our Approach:

- **Instant AI verification** (seconds)
- **Digital, tamper-proof records**
- **Individual action tracking**
- **Real-time impact dashboard**

---

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend:
- React 18, TypeScript, Vite (fast builds, HMR), TailwindCSS (utility-first styling)

### Backend & Database:
- Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime)
- Real-time subscriptions (live updates)

### Verification:
- Nominatim API (geocoding)
- Browser Geolocation API (GPS)
- Custom AI engine (future: TensorFlow)

### Maps & Location:
- Leaflet (interactive maps)
- OpenStreetMap (free tiles)
- Reverse geocoding (location names)
